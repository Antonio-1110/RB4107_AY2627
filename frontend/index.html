      Safety settings: cooking, person and timer

      1Is it cooking?

      —

      The thermal camera decides: cooking starts when the hottest spot reaches this temperature.

      More settings

      The cookware says how fast a pan loses heat. A pan that is only cooling down, or whose temperature is falling, is moving away from danger: a buzzer or shut-off that comes due then waits, up to the limits below.

      2Is someone there?

      —

      The radars decide. A short blip never restarts the countdown; only staying for the "back after" time does.

      More settings

      Radar rules (still being tuned on the bench)

      Tell someone walking past from someone cooking. "Observe" only shows the verdict below; "enforce" stops a passer-by counting as at the stove.

      3How long before…

      —

      Counted from when the person left. Hotter pans get shorter times (More settings).

          Per-gate settings (switch bands on or off, custom thresholds)

          Each gate is one distance band. Untick a band to ignore it (for example the band where a fan sits). Thresholds are 0–99, higher means less sensitive; sending them switches that sensitivity to custom. "Read from sensor" fills in the values the sensor uses now.
