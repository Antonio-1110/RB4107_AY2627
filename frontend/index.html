Cookware: how fast the pans at this stall lose heat. A pan that is only cooling down holds the buzzer and shutdown back; a lowered flame, a simmer or boiling does not. When unsure, pick the shorter time.

Person

—

The S3 decides, not the radars. A radar blip never restarts the absence count; only a confirmed return does.

AT THE STOVE (attendance)

Someone passing stops the clock while they are detected; staying the time above cancels the timeline. The pause has a budget so a busy aisle cannot stall it.

RADAR STATE AND DISTANCE (placeholder rules)

Tells someone passing from someone dwelling at the stove by how long the radar keeps a target, how much of it is stationary and how far its distance wanders. Observe only shows the verdict; enforce makes a passing target not count as at the stove.

      Per-gate settings (switch bands on or off, custom thresholds)

      Each gate is one distance band. Untick a band to ignore it (for example the band where a fan sits). Thresholds are 0–99, higher means less sensitive; sending them switches that sensitivity to custom. "Read from sensor" fills in the values the sensor uses now.
